1. Introduction

Welcome to the Kitengela Mall Parking Management System. This manual is intended to guide all users — gate staff, drivers, business owners, and administrators — through the operation of the system from day-to-day tasks to advanced administrative functions.

The system was built to address the real challenges that come with running a busy multi-level parking facility inside a large commercial mall. Before this system was put in place, the process of tracking which bays were occupied, calculating the right fee per vehicle, collecting payment, and keeping a reliable audit trail all depended on manual effort. That approach was slow, prone to human error, and opened the door to revenue leakage. This system closes those gaps by automating every step of the parking process — from the moment a vehicle approaches the entry barrier to the moment it drives out.

The manual is organized by role and workflow so that each type of user can go straight to the section that is relevant to them. Gate operators will find Chapters 6 and 7 most useful for their daily work. Customers paying for parking should refer to Chapter 8. Administrators and supervisors should read Chapters 9 through 18 in detail.

1.1 Purpose of this Document

This document provides:

  • Clear step-by-step instructions for all system functions
  • Explanations of core business rules including pricing, grace periods, and monthly invoicing
  • Guidance on how to handle exceptions, emergency overrides, and system problems
  • Reference material that can be used for training new staff and administrators

2. System Overview

2.1 What the System Does

The Kitengela Mall Parking Management System is a web-based application that handles the complete lifecycle of a vehicle's stay in the mall's two-basement parking facility. At a high level, its core capabilities are:

  1. Vehicle Entry Management — Every vehicle entering the facility is recorded, assigned a numbered bay, and checked against the restricted list before being allowed in.
  2. Vehicle Exit and Fee Calculation — On exit, the system calculates the exact fee owed based on the duration of the visit and routes the driver through payment or a free-exit path depending on their vehicle category.
  3. M-Pesa Payment Processing — Fees are collected directly through the driver's mobile phone using Safaricom's Lipa Na M-Pesa (STK Push) service. No cash changes hands at the gate.
  4. Staff Free Parking — Registered employee vehicles are recognized automatically and released at exit without any charge.
  5. Monthly Owner Invoicing — Vehicles belonging to registered business owners or mall tenants are granted a discount and their fees are accumulated then settled on a monthly basis rather than at each exit.
  6. Bay Management — The system tracks the live occupancy of all 262 bays across both basement floors and prevents over-allocation by blocking entry when the facility is full.
  7. Restricted Vehicle Control — Plates added to the restricted list are automatically denied entry, with no opportunity for bypass at the gate level.
  8. Administrative Oversight — A dedicated admin panel gives supervisors access to live dashboards, revenue reports, user management, full audit logs, and emergency override controls.

2.2 Facility Layout

The parking facility is made up of two underground levels. Each level contains 131 individually numbered bays:

FloorBay Number RangeTotal Bays
Basement 1 (B1)B1-001 to B1-131131 bays
Basement 2 (B2)B2-001 to B2-131131 bays

Bays are assigned sequentially — the system always picks the lowest available bay number across either floor. Currently the system does not separate floor preferences.

6. Gate Operations — Vehicle Entry

6.1 Overview

Every vehicle entering the parking facility must be recorded through the entry page. This process checks whether the vehicle is permitted, finds an available bay, records the entry time, and gives gate staff the bay number to communicate to the driver. The whole check takes about one to two seconds.

6.2 Step-by-Step: Logging a Vehicle Entry

Step 1 — Open the entry page on the gate terminal. Bookmark this URL so you can access it with one tap:

http://[server-ip]/Kitengela_Parking/public/gate/entry.php

Step 2 — When a vehicle pulls up at the boom barrier, read the license plate number carefully.

Step 3 — Type the plate number into the Enter Plate Number field. The system accepts plates in any mix of upper and lowercase letters (e.g. kca 123a will be saved as KCA 123A) and handles spacing automatically.

Step 4 — Click Log Entry or press Enter.

Step 5 — Read the system response and act accordingly:

Screen ResponseMeaningWhat to Do
Green — Bay Assigned (e.g. B1-023)Entry recorded, bay availableRaise boom gate. Tell driver their bay number.
Red — Restricted VehiclePlate is on the blocked listKeep gate down. Do not allow entry. Alert supervisor.
Red — Already InsideVehicle is already logged as parkedKeep gate down. Verify plate, contact admin if needed.
Red — Parking FullAll 262 bays occupiedKeep gate down. Politely redirect driver.

Step 6 — The page resets automatically after 3 seconds, ready for the next vehicle.

6.3 Important Notes for Gate Entry Staff

Important: Never allow a vehicle in without recording it in the system, even if there is a queue. The exit fee relies entirely on having a correct entry record with an accurate entry time. If the system is temporarily unavailable, write the plate number and time on paper and log it as soon as the system is back.
  • If a driver says they were already logged in but nothing shows, verify the plate spelling carefully — a single character difference creates an entirely separate record in the database.
  • Bay numbers are assigned by the system automatically. You cannot choose or swap bays manually.

7. Gate Operations — Vehicle Exit

7.1 Overview

When a vehicle is ready to leave, the exit gate staff processes the plate number on the exit page. The system calculates the parking fee and either allows a free exit immediately or directs the driver to pay via M-Pesa before the gate opens.

7.2 Step-by-Step: Processing a Vehicle Exit

Step 1 — Open the exit page on the gate terminal:

http://[server-ip]/Kitengela_Parking/public/gate/exit.php

Step 2 — Ask the driver for their plate number and type it into the field, then click Process Exit.

Step 3 — The system will display one of the following outcomes:

Outcome DisplayedWhat It MeansAction
Free Exit — Staff VehiclePlate belongs to a registered employeeRaise boom gate immediately.
Free Exit — Grace PeriodVehicle has been parked ≤ 30 minutesRaise boom gate immediately.
Free Exit — Owner InvoicedRegistered business owner vehicleRaise boom gate. Fee is added to monthly invoice.
Payment Required — KES [amount]Standard visitor, fee is owedDirect driver to payment page or kiosk.
Plate Not FoundNo matching entry recordDo not open gate. Check plate spelling, contact admin.

Step 4 — If payment is required, the system loads the payment page for the driver automatically. Once the driver completes M-Pesa payment on their phone, the gate opens by itself — you do not need to do anything further.

8. Driver and Visitor Guide — Making a Payment

8.1 Overview

Kitengela Mall uses the Lipa Na M-Pesa system for cashless parking payment. Payment is made directly from your mobile phone. You do not need to hand over cash, and the gate will open automatically once your payment is confirmed.

8.2 Step-by-Step: Paying for Parking

Step 1 — When you are ready to leave, the gate staff will process your plate at the exit terminal. If a fee is owed, a payment screen will appear on the kiosk or tablet near the exit gate showing:

  • Your vehicle plate number
  • The amount due (in Kenyan Shillings)
  • A field for your M-Pesa phone number

Step 2 — Type in your Safaricom phone number. Any of these formats are accepted:

  • 0712 345 678
  • +254712345678
  • 254712345678

Step 3 — Click Pay Now. The system sends a payment request to Safaricom, and within a few seconds you should feel your phone vibrate or see a notification pop up.

Step 4 — On your mobile phone, a screen will appear asking you to confirm payment, similar to:

"Kitengela Parking — Pay KES [amount]. Enter M-Pesa PIN to proceed."

Enter your M-Pesa PIN and confirm.

Step 5 — The waiting screen on the kiosk/tablet will show a loading animation and the message "Waiting for payment..." Once your PIN is verified by Safaricom, the screen will automatically switch to a green success message and the boom gate will open.

Step 6 — Proceed to the exit gate. Safaricom will also send an SMS receipt to your phone with a transaction reference number (e.g. RKA1234XYZ) — keep this for your records.

8.3 What to Do if Payment Fails

If the M-Pesa prompt does not appear on your phone, or you dismissed it accidentally, or your phone was off:

  1. Wait for the kiosk screen to show the payment result (usually within 60 seconds).
  2. If it shows "Payment Failed" or "Timed Out", click the Retry Payment button to send a new prompt to your phone.
  3. There is a 30-second cooldown between retries to prevent double deductions.
  4. If the problem persists after two attempts, call a gate attendant. The administrator can manually release your vehicle while payment is sorted separately.

8.4 Frequently Asked Questions

QuestionAnswer
Can I pay with cash?No. The system is fully cashless and uses M-Pesa only.
Will I get a receipt?Yes. Safaricom sends an SMS receipt to your phone with the transaction reference number after successful payment.
What if I entered my PIN wrongly?A wrong PIN cancels the transaction — no money is deducted. You can retry from the waiting page.
I was parked under 30 minutes but was asked to pay?This can happen if you had an older unclosed session. Speak to gate staff to investigate.
Can I leave and pay later?No. The gate will not open until payment is confirmed or an administrator manually overrides it.

12. Admin Panel — Staff Vehicle Management

Staff vehicles belong to mall employees or other approved personnel who are entitled to park free of charge at any time. Once a plate is added to the staff list, the system will recognize it at exit and apply a zero fee, regardless of how long the vehicle was parked.

Adding a Staff Vehicle

  1. In the admin navigation, click Staff Vehicles.
  2. In the Add Staff Vehicle form, enter the plate number and the employee name.
  3. Click Add Vehicle.

The vehicle will be active immediately. The next time this plate exits the facility, no fee will be charged.

Reminder: Only add vehicles that genuinely belong to current, active employees. Free parking is a staff benefit and should not be applied to personal or family vehicles that are not authorized.

Removing a Staff Vehicle

When an employee leaves the organization:

  1. Find the entry in the staff vehicles list.
  2. Click Remove.

The record is soft-deleted — it is hidden from the active list but stays in the database for audit purposes. A super admin can restore it or permanently delete it if needed. If the employee's vehicle is currently inside the facility, they will be charged normally on their next exit following removal.

Public Staff Parking Display

Staff vehicles currently inside the facility are shown on the public display page at .../public/staff.php. This is useful for security desks or managers checking who is on-site.

13. Admin Panel — Owner Vehicle Management and Monthly Billing

How Owner Accounts Work

Mall tenants and registered corporate clients are set up as owner accounts. Rather than paying at each exit via M-Pesa, their fees are accumulated throughout the month and settled periodically. A 30% discount applies to the full calculated fee, and only the discounted amount (70% of the fee) is invoiced.

When an owner vehicle exits:

  • The system calculates the normal parking fee.
  • It records it as invoiced (not collected by M-Pesa).
  • The discounted total accumulates under that owner's billing ledger.
  • The gate opens without any payment being requested.

Adding a Business Owner Vehicle

  1. Click Owners in the admin navigation.
  2. Fill in the Add Business Owner Vehicle form with the plate number and owner name.
  3. Make sure the Invoice Monthly checkbox is checked.
  4. Click Add Owner.

Viewing the Billing Summary

The owners page shows a table with a billing summary for every registered owner:

ColumnWhat It Shows
Plate NumberThe owner's vehicle registration
Owner NameThe business or individual name
Nominal Fee (KES)Total of all parking fees accumulated since last payment (before discount)
Total Due (KES)70% of nominal fee — the actual amount the owner owes after the 30% discount
Due PeriodThe current billing month

Computing Monthly Totals

Before issuing an invoice or recording a payment, always click Compute Totals first. This recalculates the accumulated fees and discounts for all owner accounts to make sure the figures are current and accurate.

Recording a Payment

When an owner settles their account:

  1. Click Compute Totals to refresh the billing figures.
  2. Find the owner in the table and click Record Payment next to their entry.
  3. Confirm the action when prompted.

The system will mark all outstanding invoiced records for that plate as paid, set the outstanding balance to zero, and advance the billing period to the following month.

Removing an Owner Account

If a tenant's account should no longer receive the monthly invoicing benefit, click Remove next to their entry. Their vehicle will be charged normal visitor rates from the next exit. Super admins can restore or permanently delete the record.

14. Admin Panel — Restricted Vehicles

The restricted vehicles list is a blocklist that prevents specific vehicles from entering the parking facility. Any plate on this list will be refused at the entry gate — the system will display a denial message and the gate will not open.

When to Restrict a Vehicle

Common reasons for adding a vehicle to the restricted list include:

  • Repeated parking without payment or deliberate attempts to evade fees
  • Vehicle involved in a theft, fraud, or security incident at the mall
  • Direct instruction from mall management or security

Adding a Restricted Vehicle

  1. Click Restricted Vehicles in the admin navigation.
  2. Enter the plate number and a brief reason for the restriction.
  3. Click Add to Restricted List.

The restriction takes effect immediately. The next time this plate attempts entry, it will be blocked.

Removing a Restriction

If a restriction should be lifted, click Remove next to the entry. The plate goes to a soft-delete recycle bin. A super admin can restore it if it was removed in error or permanently delete the record.

17. Admin Panel — Database Tools

Super Admin access only. Use with extreme care. Some operations cannot be undone.

Database Search

The database search tool lets you look up records across any table in the system. This is useful for resolving payment disputes, verifying a plate's full parking history, or tracing a specific M-Pesa transaction reference.

  1. Click Database Tools in the admin navigation.
  2. Select the table you want to search from the dropdown.
  3. Enter a search term (e.g. a plate number, receipt number, or username).
  4. Click Search. Matching rows are shown in a results table below.

Clearing Vehicle Logs

Over time, the vehicle logs table grows large. Periodic clearing keeps the system running smoothly. Before any data is deleted, the system automatically:

  1. Calculates the total revenue contained in the current logs.
  2. Saves that figure to the revenue_archive table along with the date, your username, and how many records were cleared.
  3. Only then removes the old records.

This means the cumulative revenue figure on the dashboard is never reduced by clearing — historical earnings are always preserved in the archive.

To clear vehicle logs:

  1. Click Clear Vehicle Logs on the Database Tools page.
  2. Read the warning carefully.
  3. Confirm twice when prompted.
Warning: Clearing logs is irreversible. Individual vehicle records will be permanently removed. Only the aggregate revenue total is archived. Do not clear logs unless you are certain you no longer need the individual records.

20. Manual Gate Bypass (Emergency Override)

What Is a Bypass?

A manual gate bypass is an emergency function available on the Admin Dashboard. It allows an administrator to forcibly release a vehicle from the system without collecting M-Pesa payment. The gate staff is then able to raise the boom gate manually. This function exists for genuine emergencies only and every use is permanently recorded in the audit trail.

When to Use a Bypass

Appropriate situations include:

  • The driver cannot pay via M-Pesa (no balance, phone broken, Safaricom network outage)
  • A medical or security emergency requires the vehicle to leave immediately
  • A vehicle was logged with the wrong plate number and the record needs to be cleared
  • The M-Pesa service is completely down and no alternative payment is available
Important: Every bypass is permanently written into the audit log with your name, IP address, and timestamp. Bypasses are irrevocable. Misuse of this feature is detectable and should be treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

How to Perform a Manual Bypass — Step by Step

  1. Log in to the Admin Panel and go to the Dashboard.
  2. Find the vehicle in the Active Vehicles table.
  3. Click the Manual Bypass button in the Actions column for that row.
  4. A first confirmation dialog will appear — read it carefully and click Yes, proceed.
  5. A second confirmation dialog will appear as a final check.
  6. Confirm to proceed.

The system will immediately record the current time as the vehicle's exit time, mark the record with a bypass flag, free the parking bay, and write the full event to the audit log. Inform gate staff to raise the barrier for the vehicle.

Bypass Outcomes by Scenario

Scenario at the Time of BypassWhat the System Does

Vehicle has already exitedEmergency override flag stamped on the most recent log record
Vehicle has no record in the system at all"Emergency Access" record created and logged

After a Bypass

The revenue report will tag this record as a bypass rather than a collected payment. If the amount is owed by the driver, pursue collection through the mall's standard debt recovery process. The audit log entry will tell you exactly which admin authorized the bypass and at what time, which is useful for any follow-up investigation.

21. M-Pesa Payment Integration — Technical Overview

This chapter is intended for technical staff and administrators who need to understand the M-Pesa payment flow for troubleshooting purposes.

21.1 What Is M-Pesa STK Push?

STK Push — short for SIM Toolkit Push — is a Safaricom service that sends a payment approval prompt directly to a customer's mobile phone at the request of a business. The customer sees a pop-up on their screen asking them to enter their M-Pesa PIN to approve the payment. The entire interaction happens through the customer's SIM card and requires no app download — it works on any M-Pesa-enabled Safaricom line.

21.2 Payment Flow

The diagram below shows how a standard M-Pesa payment moves through the system from the moment the driver clicks "Pay Now" to the moment the gate opens.

↓

↓ NO (Cancelled or timeout)

↓

↓

21.3 Callback Security

The "callback" is the notification Safaricom sends back to the system to report the payment result. For this to work, the system's callback URL must be reachable from the internet — it cannot be on a private, non-routable network address. During development and testing, a tool called ngrok is used to create a temporary public tunnel to the local server. In a production deployment, the server should be assigned a public IP or hostname.

The callback URL can be set using the MPESA_CALLBACK_URL environment variable on the server.

21.5 Retry Logic

The system automatically retries failed API network requests on its own — up to two retries on connection timeouts and one retry on Safaricom server errors. From the driver's side, a 30-second cooldown is enforced between manual retry attempts to ensure Safaricom does not process two deductions for the same session.
